Vanilla gives you a bed, a crafting table and... that's about it for furnishing a home.
**Fantasy's Furniture** adds **matching furniture sets** so your base actually looks lived-in —
chairs you can sit in, tables, drawers with real storage, lamps, shelves and more.

<p class="sb-eyebrow">What you get</p>

- **Themed sets** that share a look, so a room feels designed instead of thrown together.
- **Functional pieces** — drawers and cabinets store items; chairs let you sit; lamps light the
  place up.
- **An easy way to make a house a home** without needing a redstone degree.
