A whole **bestiary of new undead and nightmare creatures** that turns nightfall back into something
to fear. Shambling corpses, screaming wraiths, fire-spitting horrors and hulking brutes prowl the
dark — far more varied and aggressive than vanilla's handful of zombies and skeletons.

<p class="sb-eyebrow">What to expect</p>

- **New night-time mobs** with their own attacks and behaviours — some swarm, some hit like a truck,
  some you really don't want to corner you.
- **New weapons and gear** crafted from what they drop — scythes, blades and grim trinkets that turn
  a dangerous night into a payday.
- **Themed encounters** that make every sunset a small decision: push on, or hole up.

!!! warning "Night is no longer 'just spawn a few zombies'"
    Early on, get a **light source, a weapon and walls** before dark. Pair it with
    **[Zombie Awareness](zombie-awareness.md)** and the dark becomes a genuine threat — that's the
    point.
