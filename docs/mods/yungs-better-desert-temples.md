The vanilla desert pyramid is one room and a hole in the floor. **Better Desert Temples** replaces it
with a sprawling, **trap-filled tomb** — multiple floors, hidden passages, puzzles and far better
loot for anyone willing to dig in.

<p class="sb-eyebrow">What's different</p>

- **A real dungeon**, not a single chamber — winding halls, sealed rooms and secrets to find.
- **Traps and mechanisms** — pressure plates, hidden TNT and puzzles guard the good chests, so
  watch your step.
- **Better rewards** for clearing it, making the desert worth a detour again.
