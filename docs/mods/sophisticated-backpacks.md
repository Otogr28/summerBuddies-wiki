A backpack that **grows with you**. Start with a simple bag of extra slots, then slot in **upgrade
modules** to turn it into a portable workshop — the more you invest, the more it does.

<p class="sb-eyebrow">Upgrades worth knowing</p>

- **Pickup &amp; Magnet** — items go straight into the backpack as you mine and fight.
- **Smelting / Cooking** — auto-smelt ore or cook food while you carry it.
- **Crafting** — open a crafting grid right inside the bag.
- **Stack &amp; Capacity** — hold far more than a normal inventory.
- Plus **dye it, name it, lock filters**, and even **place it as a chest**.

!!! tip "It plays nicely with Traveler's Backpack"
    **[Traveler's Backpack](travelers-backpack.md)** is the big wearable pack with fluid tanks and a
    crafting bench; **Sophisticated Backpacks** is the upgrade-driven one. Use whichever fits your
    playstyle — or both.
