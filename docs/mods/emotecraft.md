Pure personality. **Emote Craft** lets your character **play animated emotes** — wave, sit, bow,
clap, dance, point — that everyone nearby sees in real time. Perfect for a co-op server where half
the fun is goofing around.

<p class="sb-eyebrow">How it works</p>

- Open the **emote wheel** with a keybind and pick one — quick to fire mid-conversation.
- Others see your emote **live**, so it's great for greetings, reactions and roleplay.
- A growing list of built-in emotes, with more you can add.

!!! tip "Made for playing together"
    Combine it with **[Voice Translate](../custom/voice-translate.md)** and a wave or a thumbs-up
    reads the same in every language — handy when your group doesn't all speak one.
