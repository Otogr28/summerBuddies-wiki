From the team behind **[Oh The Biomes We've Gone](oh-the-biomes-weve-gone.md)** — a tree overhaul that
makes forests feel **grand and alive** instead of a grid of identical lollipops. Bigger, more natural
trees with varied shapes, branches and canopies fill out the world.

<p class="sb-eyebrow">What it changes</p>

- **Grander tree generation** — sweeping shapes and sizes that give woodlands real character.
- **New tree types** that fit the modded biomes around them.
- **Little touches** like fruit and falling leaves that make a forest feel like somewhere, not just
  a wood farm.

!!! info "It pairs with the biomes"
    Designed to grow alongside **Oh The Biomes We've Gone**, so the new forests and the new biomes
    look like they belong to the same world.
