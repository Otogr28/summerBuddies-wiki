A **cozy decoration** mod all about warm, lived-in interiors. **Candlelight** brings dining and
homestead pieces — tables, stools, candles, cooking stations and food dishes — so a house feels less
like a stone box and more like a home.

<p class="sb-eyebrow">What's inside</p>

- **Dining &amp; comfort** — set tables, sit on stools, light candles and lamps.
- **Cooking &amp; food** — prep and serve new dishes for a homely kitchen.
- **Decor details** that fill the gaps vanilla leaves bare.

!!! tip "Stack your decoration mods"
    Mix with **[Fantasy's Furniture](fantasy-furniture.md)**,
    **[Supplementaries](supplementaries.md)** and **[Amendments](amendments.md)** — between them
    you can decorate just about anything, anywhere.
