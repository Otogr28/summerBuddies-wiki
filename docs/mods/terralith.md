**Terralith reshapes the overworld** — so the home realm of **Aincrad** isn't the flat, familiar
Minecraft you know. Towering mountain ranges, deep river canyons, glaciers, volcanic wastes,
skylands and lush valleys, all built from **vanilla blocks** so everything still feels like
Minecraft, just grander.

<p class="sb-eyebrow">What it adds</p>

- **100+ new biome variants** layered over the world — alpine highlands, painted deserts, mirage
  oases, ancient sand dunes, amethyst rainforests and more.
- **Dramatic terrain** — real mountains worth climbing, cliffs, caves and waterfalls that make
  exploring feel like a journey.
- **Small structures** scattered around to reward wandering off the path.

!!! info "Plays well with the other world mods"
    Terralith handles the **terrain and vanilla-style biomes**, while
    **[Oh The Biomes We've Gone](oh-the-biomes-weve-gone.md)** adds its own modded biomes, woods and
    crops on top. Together they make Aincrad enormous and varied — no two directions look the same.
